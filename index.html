Common questions

Before you rely on the date.

How does ReturnBy calculate the deadline?

ReturnBy adds the number of days you enter to the purchase or delivery date you choose. That gives you an estimate to plan around. Retailers may count days differently, use a different starting event, or apply product-specific exceptions, so confirm the store's policy before relying on the result.

Does ReturnBy upload or sell my purchase information?

No. There is no account or receipt upload. If you save a return, the information is stored in your browser on this device. ReturnBy currently does not use analytics or tracking pixels.

What does “Add calendar reminder” do?

It downloads a standard calendar file with the estimated deadline and the reminder timing you selected. You choose whether to add that file to your calendar.

Will ReturnBy tell me whether an item is actually eligible for return?

No. ReturnBy helps with date tracking only. Final eligibility, refund method, fees, exclusions, and policy exceptions are controlled by the retailer.
