Privacy

Private by design.

ReturnBy is designed to calculate and track return deadlines without requiring an account or sending your purchase details to a ReturnBy server.

What stays on your device

When you choose “Save to this device,” ReturnBy stores the return details in your browser's local storage. That can include the item name, store, price, selected dates, estimated deadline, and return status. Clearing browser storage may erase those saved returns.

What is not uploaded

ReturnBy does not ask you to upload receipts, create an account, or provide an email address. The current site does not include analytics scripts or tracking pixels.

Calendar and clipboard actions

If you add a calendar reminder, your browser creates and downloads a calendar file on your device. If you copy return details, your browser uses its clipboard feature. ReturnBy does not send those details to a ReturnBy server.

External links

The optional “Buy me a coffee” support link opens an external service. That service has its own privacy practices and policies.

Policy changes

If ReturnBy later adds analytics, accounts, cloud sync, or another service that changes how information is handled, this page should be updated before those features are enabled.
